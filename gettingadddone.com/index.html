Frequently asked

Do I need Notion Pro?

No. Everything works with the free Notion plan.

Yes. Once duplicated, it's your workspace. Add, rename, or remove anything.

Planner vs. Planner Pro?

Planner has Projects + Tasks with ICE scoring. Pro adds the Focus Log, which shows where your hours actually go.

Is this just another GTD system?

Inspired by GTD, redesigned for ADD brains. Tickler dates, energy matching, Signal/Noise. No weekly reviews that never happen.
